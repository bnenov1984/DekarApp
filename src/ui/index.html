Method

Nothing is a number until the sheet has a scale.

The order is fixed on purpose. Dekar keeps the scale declaration visible instead of burying it in a settings dialog.

01

Import the sheet

Drop a PNG or JPG of the plan onto the page, or start on a blank canvas. PDF pages and map views follow the same path.

02

Calibrate against one known dimension

Click both ends of a length you trust and type its value. Every area, perimeter and distance on the sheet now derives from that one declaration. Change it, and all of them update together.

REF 24.00 m = 576 px
1 px = 0.0417 m · scale 1 : 500
03

Trace, split, read

Trace the boundary. Divide it into lots with one line. Move a shared corner and both neighbours follow in a single undoable step. Figures update as you draw.

Capabilities

Built for people who answer for the figure.

Brokers, surveyors and developers measuring from plans at a desk. No CAD training assumed; CAD-grade rules kept.

Scale per sheet

Scale is a property of the drawing, not of each shape. Recalibration is atomic: all measurements on the sheet change together or not at all.

INV-3 · Measurement Engine

Split with conserved total

One line divides a parcel into parts that cover the whole without gaps or overlap. Their areas sum to the parent, and keep summing after you move a corner.

PD-009 · PD-021

Linked vertices

Touching lots share their boundary. Dragging the common vertex moves both; Ctrl-drag detaches one. An edit that would fold a part inside out is refused.

PD-024 · PD-025

Registered area

Enter the official area from the deed. Geometry stays as drawn, the parts share the registered total, and the report shows which figure is which.
